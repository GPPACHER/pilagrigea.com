Reading the dial

Each dot is that venue's current local time on its ring. Bright + halo = in session; dim = closed.

The black track = session overlap — 2+ venues open at once (e.g. LDN∩NYSE 21:30–00:30).

The yellow track = volatility triggers — funds/algos loading, spreads wide, traps form.
Bright slots = do-not-trade windows (cash open, stop-runs, FOMC, MOC/close).
Regular slots = reduced size (reversals, flips, lunch traps, power hour).
